Tips for Effective Bug Reporting

  • Be Specific: Use clear, descriptive titles that summarize the issue
  • Steps to Reproduce: List the exact steps that lead to the bug
  • Expected vs Actual: Describe what should happen vs what actually happens
  • Environment Details: Include browser, OS, or other relevant environment information
  • Screenshots: If applicable, describe what visual elements are affected
  • Error Messages: Include any error messages or codes you encountered
